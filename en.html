Where's My Type — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026 (previous: November 1, 2026) · 한국어

Where's My Type (the “App”, formerly “Walk Together”) lets adults aged 19 and over create a profile, set the kind of person they want to meet, find nearby matches on a map and chat one-to-one. We process only the information the service needs.

Location: we never store exact coordinates. Only a value rounded to about 1 km is sent to the server, and the map shows you slightly away from even that point. Turning on “Hide me from the map” erases your location and removes you from other people's maps. If you deny location permission you can still use the App by moving the map yourself. Showing your location on other users' maps is optional consent; without it you can still view the map and chat.

We do not ask for or store religion, ethnicity, political views, health information or sexual orientation.

2. Advertising

The App is free and shows Google AdMob ads. AdMob may collect the advertising ID, device information, approximate location and app usage to serve and measure ads. See Google's advertising policies. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your device settings.

4. What other users can see

Other logged-in users can see your profile (nickname, gender, age, height, weight if entered, drinking, smoking, MBTI, look, foods and interests, times, about me), your approximate location on the map (unless hidden) and your online status. Chat messages are visible only to the other person in the chat. Your match preferences, friends and block lists and email address are not shown to other users. People you block cannot see you on the map or in lists.

5. Deleting your account and data

  • In the App: tap “Me → Delete account”. It takes effect immediately and deletes your profile, match preferences, location, consent records, the one-to-one chats you are in and their messages, friends and block records, and new-match alert settings (approximate location and push token).
  • Without the App: email us from, or naming, the email address of your account and ask for deletion. We handle requests within 7 days.

Report records, including the chat messages saved with a report, are kept for 1 year after handling to prevent misuse, even if the account is deleted, then deleted.

6. Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your information, or ask us to stop processing it, at any time. You can edit your profile and match preferences under “Me”; send any other request to the contact below. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under the GDPR, or California under the CCPA), you may also have the right to data portability and to complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Age requirement

The App is only for people aged 19 or older. We ask for your birth year and a confirmation that you are 19 or older at sign-up. This currently relies on your own confirmation; we do not verify identity.
